\section{An equally informed positive first-correction prior}
Let $R_1=R_2+UP'(m)$ and let $c_1=\mathbb E_Q[UP'(m)]$ be the exact
Gaussian-tilt coefficient already supplied to the second-correction method.
For $M_2\geq\|C_2\|_\infty$, define
$B_Q=M_2\mathbb E_Q U^2/2$. The global integral-remainder bound gives
$|\mathbb E_Q R_2|\leq B_Q$. For a convex ordinary call it also gives
$\mathbb E_Q R_2\geq0$. Hence an outward interval $[c_{1,-},c_{1,+}]$
provides continuous first-mean bounds
\[
 [L_Q,U_Q]=
 \begin{cases}
 [c_{1,-}-B_Q,c_{1,+}+B_Q],&\text{capped call},\\
 [c_{1,-},c_{1,+}+B_Q],&\text{ordinary call}.
 \end{cases}
\]
The exact centring and positive weights give $U\geq0$; the monotone tested
payoffs give $R_1\geq0$. Suppose the price-independent first-residual
financial transfer proves
$|\mathbb E_Q R_1-\alpha\mathbb E_N R_1|\leq\beta_1$, with certified
$0<\alpha_-\leq\alpha\leq\alpha_+$. On the finite support $R_1\leq B_1$.
Then
\[
 L_N=\max\{0,L_Q-\beta_1\}/\alpha_+,\qquad
 U_N=\min\{B_1,(U_Q+\beta_1)/\alpha_-,U_{N,\rm previous}\}
\]
bound the exact finite first-correction mean. For the lower bound the
positive part is divided by the largest mass; for the upper bound the
nonnegative upper endpoint is divided by the smallest mass. Intersection
with a separately proved upper mean is valid. All endpoints remain outward.

If the represented probability differs pointwise from $R_1/B_1$ by at most
$\eta_p$, its mean belongs to
\[
 [\ell,u]=[\max\{0,L_N/B_1-\eta_p\},
 \min\{1,U_N/B_1+\eta_p,u_{\rm previous}\}].
\]
An exact rational midpoint requires no quantum estimation if its monetary
radius $\alpha_+B_1(u-\ell)/2$, added to the complete deterministic budget,
meets the requested tolerance. Otherwise the canonical amplitude plan uses
$u$ for its Bernoulli variance envelope and retains the same full circuit,
Fourier and confidence charges. The lower probability endpoint is not
misused as a Bernoulli variance; it supports only valid interval stopping.
The deterministic budget already encloses continuous-to-finite transfer
and arithmetic, so charging the corresponding allowances again inside this
prior can be conservative but cannot invalidate the final price certificate.

The full Gaussian $\mathbb E_Q U^2$ is evaluated by finite multinomial
Gaussian exponential moments, preserving every covariance term. Thus neither
an observed option price nor a numerical basket-price answer enters this
prior. This is a project-specific information-sharing improvement derived
from the established conditional control and a proved remainder bound,
rather than a claim to invent Taylor bounds or probability intervals.

\section{A shared continuous screen before quantum evaluation}
The same c0+c1 and B_Q determine a continuous price interval. For a capped
call it is [c0+c1-B_Q,c0+c1+B_Q] intersected with [0,H]; for an ordinary
call it is [c0+c1,c0+c1+B_Q] intersected with the nonnegative half-line.
Every coefficient endpoint is enlarged outwards before these intersections.
The global remainder theorem proves containment directly, without a
financial grid, mass approximation or quantum circuit. Its exact rational
midpoint has error no larger than half the interval width. If that radius
meets the undiscounted target, every competing implementation receives the
same zero-quantum decision. The underlying estimator plans remain retained
as conditional diagnostics and are not counted as quantum gains.
