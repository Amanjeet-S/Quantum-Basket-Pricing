# 2 Checked positive result

The shared threshold preparation computes one nonnegative correction flag using the same strike threshold and Gaussian draw at its geometric and arithmetic endpoints. It removes two conditional-price lookup evaluations, retains normaliser one in the cap-one implementation and reduces the reflected core by fourteen wires. All financial grid, normal CDF, strike midpoint, endpoint arithmetic, clean-work and finite-confidence obligations are included.

It is neither an intrinsic amplitude-estimation query improvement nor a physical quantum advantage.

Independent checks reproduce all 64 rival configurations and twelve rejections, 1,845,728 scalar ordinates, 104 rival profiles and 104 complete confidence plans. # 3 Applicability and limits

The coupling theorem applies to a finite nonnegative threshold measure of mass H, with positive common Gaussian scale and ordered finite endpoints. It covers cash digitals and positive finite mixtures, as well as capped calls, subject to a certified preparation of the threshold law. It does not imply efficient preparation for every such measure. Signed threshold measures, infinite-mass ordinary-call representations and negative basket weights are outside its stated positive construction.

The conditional classical score is the expectation of the binary score given the financial factors. Its variance is smaller by the nonnegative expected conditional variance. Before finite approximation, both quantum encodings instead have the same global good probability and Grover angle. The current gain comes from actual preparation cost and admitted error budgets, not a change in that ideal query law.

The model remains an established classical risk-neutral GBM specification. The new quantum object is a pricing preparation and estimation composition. No REIT label, observed exotic option quote, empirical calibration, LSEG observation or quantum asset-dynamics claim is added. Existing classical certificates already price the unchanged continuous targets.
