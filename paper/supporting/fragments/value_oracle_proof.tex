\section{Exact numerical values and coherent dyadic buckets}

I distinguish a numerical-value oracle from an oracle that prepares only a
Bernoulli flag. This distinction is necessary because a variance-sensitive
mean-estimation result formulated in terms of numerical values does not
automatically apply to an already Bernoulli-encoded payoff. Throughout this
section, the previously certified finite graph determines an integer function
\(a:\mathcal L\longrightarrow\{0,\ldots,2^q\}\), where
\(\mathcal L\) is the finite uniform latent grid and \(q\geq1\).
The arithmetic, function tables and distributional approximation are fixed.
I set \(X(x)=2^{-q}a(x)\). The dyadic decomposition below is an exact
identity for this finite random variable. I do not identify that elementary
identity, the compute--copy--uncompute construction, or the established
amplitude-amplification geometry \citep{brassard} as a new theoretical
contribution.

\subsection{Clean numerical-value oracle}

Let \(B\) be the emitted financial-body computation. Its computational-basis
action, on initially clean private work, has the form
\[
 B\lvert x\rangle\lvert0\rangle_W
 =\lvert x\rangle\lvert h_x\rangle_W,
\]
where a declared \((q+1)\)-bit subregister of \(h_x\) contains \(a(x)\).
The remaining body history is retained until it is uncomputed. Every
arithmetic atom is an exact Boolean permutation. Every lookup macro has the
phase-correct logical XOR action proved in the instrument section, whose
published lookup and temporary-AND antecedents are
\citet{babbush} and \citet{gidney}, including
arbitrary coherent dirty output registers. Its private selector work is
reset by the corrected erasure. Each measurement-record Kraus operator is a
scalar, independent of its logical input, times that XOR action. These facts
also hold when the logical input is entangled with an external reference.
Consequently, composing the body implements the corresponding coherent
logical map without revealing \(x\) or any numerical intermediate.

Allocate a distinct \((q+1)\)-bit target \(Z\) before allocating the body
work. Copy the value subregister to \(Z\) using one CNOT per value bit, then
apply the logical inverse of \(B\). In the measured architecture, a lookup
inverse is a fresh corrected XOR instrument. It is not a reversal of a
measurement record. On basis states the resulting value oracle is
\[
 V\lvert x\rangle\lvert z\rangle_Z\lvert0\rangle_W
 =\lvert x\rangle\lvert z\mathbin{\mathrm{XOR}}a(x)\rangle_Z
   \lvert0\rangle_W.                                      \tag{V1}
\]
The body is independent of \(z\), and the copy leaves the body value
unchanged. The inverse therefore clears exactly the same history that the
forward body produced. This proves (V1) for every dirty target \(z\), not
merely \(z=0\). By linearity and the input-independent Kraus factors,
(V1) holds on arbitrary coherent superpositions with an external reference.
The logical action is an XOR involution on the clean-work subspace. A fresh
instance implements \(V^\dagger\) there. These claims do not assert that
the operational measurement tape is unitary on arbitrary dirty private work.

\subsection{A fully specified bucket predicate}

For \(b\in\{0,\ldots,q-1\}\), define
\[
 D_b(a)=\boldsymbol1\{2^b\leq a<2^{b+1}\},\qquad
 g_b(a,u)=D_b(a)\boldsymbol1\{u<a\},
 \quad 0\leq u<2^{b+1}.
\]
I use a distinct \((b+1)\)-bit comparison register for \(u\). Write
\(a=\sum_{j=0}^q2^j a_j\). Membership is equivalent to
\(a_b=1\) and \(a_{b+1}=\cdots=a_q=0\). I temporarily negate the
\(q-b\) higher bits and compute the conjunction of
\(a_b,\ldots,a_q\) into a clean ladder of \(q-b\) targets.
This requires \(q-b\) exact Toffoli gates. The lowest \((b+1)\)
bits of \(a\) are unaffected by those negations. I compute the borrow bit
of the unsigned subtraction
\(u-(a\bmod2^{b+1})\) using the existing explicitly emitted ripple
construction. A Toffoli controlled by membership and borrow XORs
\(g_b\) into the good flag. I then reverse the complete predicate history,
excluding that final flag operation.

The unsigned borrow is one precisely when
\(u<a\bmod2^{b+1}\). On the membership branch, \(a<2^{b+1}\),
so this is \(u<a\). On every other branch, the membership control is
zero. Thus the flag is correct even when the truncated comparison alone
has no financial meaning. The inverse clears every predicate carry,
conjunction target and temporary negation, while preserving \(a\), \(u\)
and the marked flag. The predicate therefore has the exact logical action
\[
 P_b\lvert a,u,f\rangle\lvert0\rangle_J
 =\lvert a,u,f\mathbin{\mathrm{XOR}}g_b(a,u)\rangle
   \lvert0\rangle_J.                                      \tag{V2}
\]
For the top bucket, set \(D_q(a)=\boldsymbol1\{a=2^q\}\).
The certified range \(0\leq a\leq2^q\) implies \(D_q(a)=a_q\).
I therefore use a single CNOT from \(a_q\) to the flag, with no comparison
register and no conjunction ladder. On basis states outside the certified
range, this circuit remains a Boolean permutation, but its financial
top-bucket interpretation is not asserted.

In the lower buckets, the ripple construction uses three Toffoli gates,
four CNOT gates and one X gate per bit in its forward history. That history
and the membership ladder are reversed after marking the flag. Hence the
exact predicate counts are
\[
 N_{\mathrm{CCX}}(P_b)=2(q-b)+6(b+1)+1=2q+4b+7,
 \quad N_{\mathrm{CX}}(P_b)=8(b+1),
 \quad N_{\mathrm X}(P_b)=2(q+1).                          \tag{V3}
\]
The predicate uses \(q+2b+4\) clean temporary wires beyond its value,
comparison and flag registers under the declared ripple construction.
At the top bucket, the counts are one CNOT and no Toffoli or X gates.
These are construction-specific counts, not lower bounds or optimality claims.

\subsection{Exact Born probabilities and price transfer}

Apply Hadamards to all latent input bits and, for \(b<q\), to all
\((b+1)\) comparison bits. Compute the financial body, apply (V2),
and uncompute the financial body. All body and predicate work returns to
zero. Denote the resulting preparation by \(A_b\). For a fixed latent
state \(x\) in bucket \(b\), exactly \(a(x)\) of the
\(2^{b+1}\) equally weighted comparison integers satisfy \(u<a(x)\).
The Born probability of the good flag is therefore exactly
\[
 p_b=\mathbb E\!\left[
    \frac{a}{2^{b+1}}D_b(a)\right],\quad 0\leq b<q,
 \qquad p_q=\mathbb P(a=2^q).                             \tag{V4}
\]
No arbitrary rotation, continuous probability oracle or free QRAM is used.
Each nonzero integer below \(2^q\) has a unique highest set bit.
Zero belongs to none of the buckets and contributes zero. Consequently,
with \(r_b=2^{b+1-q}\) for \(b<q\) and \(r_q=1\),
\[
 \mathbb E X=\sum_{b=0}^{q}r_b p_b.                       \tag{V5}
\]
This is a finite exact decomposition, including the mass at one. Any
statistical intervals for the \(p_b\) can be propagated through this
nonnegative linear identity. The already proved deterministic currency
error between the continuous financial expectation and \(\mathbb E X\)
is unchanged by (V5); partitioning the same finite values adds no bias.

\subsection{Coherent amplification and the two oracle cost models}

After \(A_b\), the financial and predicate work is clean. The invariant
subspace consists of the latent, comparison and flag registers with that
work fixed to zero. Let \(S_0=I-2\lvert0\rangle\langle0\rvert\)
on those active logical registers and let \(S_g\) negate the good flag.
Then \(Q_b=-A_b S_0 A_b^\dagger S_g\) has the usual two-dimensional
amplification action. To make the probability identity explicit, for
\(0<p_b<1\) define the normalised bad and good projections
\(\lvert B\rangle\) and \(\lvert G\rangle\) of
\(A_b\lvert0\rangle\), and set
\(\theta=\arcsin\sqrt{p_b}\). In the ordered basis
\((\lvert B\rangle,\lvert G\rangle)\),
\[
 A_b\lvert0\rangle=
 \begin{pmatrix}\cos\theta\\\sin\theta\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad
 Q_b=\begin{pmatrix}\cos2\theta&-\sin2\theta\\
                    \sin2\theta& \cos2\theta\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Multiplying this rotation \(k\) times proves that the flag probability
after \(Q_b^kA_b\) is \(\sin^2((2k+1)\theta)\).
The endpoint cases follow directly or by continuity. A controlled iterate
uses the chronological gates
\(\mathrm{CZ}_{c,f}\), \(A_b^\dagger\), controlled \(S_0\),
\(A_b\), and \(Z_c\). At control zero the two preparations cancel.
At control one, \(Z_c\) supplies the conditional global minus. Only the
reflections need added controls; the financial arithmetic is not promoted
to multiply controlled gates. Corrected lookup instruments are valid in
these coherent compositions because every measurement-record branch is
proportional to the same logical map, including on entangled inputs.

Let \(T_B\) be the fully charged T count of one financial body, including
its instantiated lookup architecture. The separately available value oracle
has \(T_V=2T_B\) and \((q+1)\) additional value-copy CNOT gates.
The fused known-body preparation computes the body once, marks the bucket,
then uncomputes that body. Under the declared seven-T exact Toffoli
decomposition, (V3) gives
\[
 T_{A_b}=2T_B+7(2q+4b+7)\quad(b<q),\qquad T_{A_q}=2T_B.     \tag{V6}
\]
In contrast, treating \(V\) as a numerical-value black box and emitting
\(V;P_b;V^\dagger\) gives
\[
 T_{A_b}^{\mathrm{blackbox}}=4T_B+7(2q+4b+7)\quad(b<q),
 \qquad T_{A_q}^{\mathrm{blackbox}}=4T_B,                    \tag{V7}
\]
with two value-copy sets of CNOT gates and a held value register.
I retain both cost models. A numerical-value query theorem cannot silently
receive the lower fused cost without assuming access to the financial body.

If \(L\) is the number of latent input bits, the lower-bucket reflection
acts on \(L+b+2\) logical qubits. Its clean conjunction ladder uses
\(2(L+b)\) exact Toffoli gates. Thus
\[
 T_{Q_b}=2T_{A_b}+14(L+b)\quad(b<q),\qquad
 T_{Q_q}=2T_{A_q}+14(L-1).                                 \tag{V8}
\]
The uniform Hadamard counts per preparation are \(L+b+1\) and \(L\),
respectively. These formulas retain all body, predicate and reflection
inverse gates. In the measured lookup mode, each selector erasure also
contributes a measurement, conditional CZ and conditional reset X; all
are multiplied by the actual number of forward and inverse body uses.
The final flag measurement and whole-shot reinitialisation remain separate
from the oracle-internal counts. Physical measurement latency, error
correction overhead and hardware execution time are not inferred from
logical T counts.

The retained resource audit expands each exact CCX into its actual seven-T
gate sequence. It reports the literal dependency schedule, a conservative
five-layer-per-CCX serial bound, and the historical assigned three-layer
profile separately. It independently emits both directions of each cached
financial block, so equality of forward and inverse depth is not assumed.
For production graphs, the counts compose fully emitted blocks and the
fully emitted predicate; they do not require retaining a full production
gate tape. Complete small-graph tapes are compared against those block
counts, and their Boolean and state-vector semantics are retained.

\subsection{Fair single-flag range and clipping opponents}

The dyadic construction must be compared with a competent single-flag
construction supplied with the same certified information. Suppose first
that a separate deterministic certificate proves \(a\leq2^c\) on every
latent grid state, with \(0\leq c\leq q\). For \(c>0\), I prepare
\(c\) uniform comparison bits and append a clean zero high bit. I compare
this \((c+1)\)-bit integer with the lowest \((c+1)\) value bits.
The zero extension is essential at the possible endpoint \(a=2^c\).
The resulting Born probability is \(p_c=\mathbb E[a]/2^c\), and
\(\mathbb E X=2^{c-q}p_c\). At \(c=0\), the range promise is
\(a\in\{0,1\}\); a single CNOT from the value bit gives this
probability without comparison work. Under the declared ripple construction,
the predicate uses \(6(c+1)\) exact Toffoli gates for \(c>0\),
and none for \(c=0\). It has no approximation or clipping bias when its
range certificate is valid. That certificate is a support bound, not a
mean or variance estimate, and is retained separately from the circuit counter.

A second opponent does not require a smaller support promise. Prepare
\(c\) uniform comparison bits and pad them with \(q+1-c\) clean zero
bits. Compare this full \((q+1)\)-bit integer with all the value bits.
For every \(a\in\{0,\ldots,2^q\}\), including \(c=0\),
the number of comparison integers below \(a\) is
\(\min(a,2^c)\). Setting \(\rho=2^{c-q}\) therefore gives
\[
 p_c^{\mathrm{clip}}=\frac{\mathbb E[\min(X,\rho)]}{\rho}.
                                                               \tag{V9}
\]
The numerical payoff register is not explicitly clamped. The finite
comparison creates the clipping exactly. Its predicate uses
\(6(q+1)\) Toffoli gates, \(8(q+1)+1\) CNOT gates and
\(2(q+1)\) X gates for every \(c\). The clean padding is private
work and is excluded from the logical all-zero reflection. The uniform
Hadamard and reflection costs decrease with \(c\), while the financial
body and full comparison counts remain charged. This opponent is valid
on the entire certified original value range. Its omitted excess-tail mean
must be included in the error allowance; it cannot be declared an unbiased
estimator of the unclipped payoff.

For completeness, an elementary sharp pointwise second-moment bound supplies
one such allowance. Let \(0\leq X\leq R\), \(R>0\), and assume
that a proved bound \(s_2\geq\mathbb E[X^2]\) is available. For
\(0<\rho<R\), the largest ratio
\((x-\rho)_+/x^2\) on \(0\leq x\leq R\) is
\[
 \gamma_R(\rho)=
 \begin{cases}
   (4\rho)^{-1},&2\rho\leq R,\\
   (R-\rho)/R^2,&\rho<R<2\rho.
 \end{cases}                                                \tag{V10}
\]
Indeed, the ratio is zero up to \(\rho\), and its derivative above
\(\rho\) is \((2\rho-x)/x^3\). It increases up to \(2\rho\)
and then decreases, proving both cases by maximisation. Equivalently, the
first case follows from \((x-2\rho)^2\geq0\). If
\(\rho\geq R\), the excess tail is identically zero. Thus
\(\tau=\mathbb E[(X-\rho)_+]\leq\gamma_R(\rho)s_2\)
when \(\rho<R\). For a certified mean bound
\(m\geq\mathbb E X\), the pointwise inequality
\((x-\rho)_+\leq(1-\rho/R)x\) also gives
\(\tau\leq(1-\rho/R)m\). The minimum of these valid bounds may be
used. Here \(s_2\) is a second-moment bound, not a variance bound;
substituting \(\operatorname{Var}(X)\) for \(\mathbb E[X^2]\)
would be incorrect. I do not claim these pointwise bounds as original.

If \(0\leq\tau\leq U_\tau\) is certified and a statistical event gives
\(\lvert\widehat p-p_c^{\mathrm{clip}}\rvert\leq\varepsilon_c\),
then
\[
 \left\lvert\rho\widehat p+\frac{U_\tau}{2}-\mathbb E X\right\rvert
 \leq\rho\varepsilon_c+\frac{U_\tau}{2}.                  \tag{V11}
\]
To prove this, write \(\mathbb E X=\rho p_c^{\mathrm{clip}}+\tau\),
subtract, and use
\(\lvert U_\tau/2-\tau\rvert\leq U_\tau/2\) with the triangle
inequality. Multiplying by the residual currency scale and adding the
previous deterministic numerical allowance gives a complete price-error
bound on the same statistical event. The midpoint tail correction is
therefore explicit; it neither estimates the tail nor silently drops its
uncertainty. Any claimed dyadic advantage must survive comparison with
these range and clipping opponents using the same proved moments.

\subsection{Reusing previously emitted financial bodies}

For the production study, I recover the primitive multiset of a forward
financial body by summing the retained node-block multisets of the earlier
resource profiles. Before reusing it, I independently emit the original
\((q+1)\)-bit comparison. Doubling the recovered body multiset and adding
that comparison must reproduce the complete retained original flag profile,
gate by gate and in its T count. The source profile hash is retained.
Only after this equality passes do I attach a freshly emitted dyadic,
range or clipped-range predicate and its reflection. Every arithmetic gate
is self-inverse, and a fresh phase-correct logical XOR lookup has exactly
the same primitive multiset as its forward lookup. Hence doubling the
body multiset is an exact count for its forward and logical inverse,
without assuming any symmetry of optimised dependency depths.

I use a deliberately conservative depth bound for this reuse path. Each
body T or T-dagger contributes one serial T layer, and each CCX contributes
the four layers of its verified literal seven-T sequence, or five layers
in the separately retained conservative schedule. Summing these quantities
is valid for either direction and for every ordering of the body atoms.
For all-gate depth, placing all expanded primitive operations in serial
order is a valid upper bound. Measured lookup erasures, feedback and resets
remain in those primitive multisets. The reusable body-space bound removes
only the original comparison and flag wires, which were reserved throughout
the original body, then adds the new predicate's reserved and private work.
It is a conservative workspace bound. Fresh complete coarse financial
circuits and small value circuits are checked against this adapter for
primitive counts, phases, clean work and quantum amplification. This
reuse method changes the resource-accounting workflow, not the quantum
oracle or the retained financial values.
