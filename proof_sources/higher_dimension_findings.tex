The Taylor calculation certifies 25 of 27 contracts overall. At the
predeclared order limit, the two unresolved controls have two assets,
$\lambda=0.05$ and caps 10 and 25. Their retained enclosures remain valid
but too wide. Existing validated one-dimensional quadrature supplies narrow
enclosures for both. Thus a deterministic continuous-model pricing answer
is available for all 27 contracts. The new run takes approximately
$15.36$ seconds on the retained host, comprising about $6.67$ seconds of
geometric setup and $8.68$ seconds of kernel work. It evaluates 25,080
geometric exponential terms and 120,690 scalar kernels. Those figures include
the full order search and shared setup, exclude process startup, and are
separate from quantum logical resource units.

The independent Taylor audit checks 540 formal derivative comparisons,
60 strike-integral derivatives, 81 directly integrated coefficient
comparisons, 81 reference-price/order containment checks, all 25,080
multinomial weights and 83 covariance-exposure identities. Outward scalar
arithmetic, analytic formulae and the explicit remainder determine the
certificate; neither agreement with a reference point nor apparent order
stability is used as its error bound.

The randomised experiment executes 27 prices using 50,331,648 exact
financial outputs and 786,432 independent-shift sampling units in its ideal
randomisation model. Twenty-four runs meet the reserved statistical
allowance. All three $\lambda=0.05$, cap-25 cases reach the prespecified
maximum of 65,536 shifts. Two nevertheless have a full monetary radius
within $0.01$ because their deterministic allowances have slack; the
four-asset case has undiscounted radius approximately $0.0111767$ and
remains unresolved by this randomised procedure. The full undiscounted
monetary criterion is met by 26 of 27 outputs. The stored randomised price
intervals are discounted; the separate combined comparison reconstructs
undiscounted endpoints explicitly and reports both bases.
All 27 narrower deterministic discounted price intervals are contained in
their corresponding randomised intervals.

Independent replay verifies every randomised financial output, batch sum,
checkpoint moment and stream hash, together with 3,544 Python-integer scalar
financial checks. A separate confidence auditor checks all 227 checkpoints
using independent 300-digit calculations and exact finite probability
sums for 66 Bernoulli laws, covering 89,298 pair-type terms. The audit
supports the implementation of the proved coverage statement; the independence
of ideal shifts remains a mathematical sampling premise. The reproducible
pseudorandom seed does not prove that premise.

The matched fixed-IID opponent receives the same actual finite range and
moments. I retain all 137 conditioned-prefix plans and select before samples.
The realised randomised-net evaluator work exceeds the selected fixed-IID
planning work in all 27 cases, with ratios approximately $2.544$ to
$133.203$. Extra net generation, pair statistics and confidence construction
are reported separately. These fixed-IID counts are certified plans, not
newly executed IID streams. The adverse comparison prevents calling this
conservative randomised construction a classical speed improvement.
Other published randomised-quadrature confidence policies remain different
algorithms \citep{jainrqmc,waudbysmithramdas}.

\subsection{What the comparison establishes}

The useful mathematical object is now the explicit continuous-model
certificate in Theorem~\ref{thm:finite-gaussian-price-certificate}. It
provides a sufficient test for pricing to a stated tolerance without
quantum mean-estimation calls. Failure of that test at a chosen order does
not prove that quantum computation is necessary, that higher orders succeed,
or that a different classical method fails. The all-contract combination
with quadrature is a constructive benchmark result for this frozen Gaussian
family, rather than a universal statement about the difficulty of financial
pricing.

The previous 82.1 per cent T-cap saving remains a checked comparison between
encodings. It cannot carry an advantage claim for these contracts when a
classical price enclosure already meets the required accuracy. Nor does the
presence of fixed-point and logical-gate costs constitute a physical quantum
runtime comparison. The exact mathematical contribution candidate concerns
the evaluable coefficient, remainder and method-selection argument; published
Taylor expansions, geometric controls and basket bounds remain essential
predecessors \citep{ju,kornzeytun,caldana,jiprezedate}.
The consulted sources distinguish their constructions, but do not establish
absence of an equivalent certificate from all earlier literature.
The full Ju article and compatible modern quantum algorithms remain material
comparison gaps. A positive literature claim requires completing those
specific comparisons, rather than adding model names or repeating the
resource saving as if it were an algorithmic breakthrough.
