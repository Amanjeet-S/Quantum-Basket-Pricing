# 3. Implementable competitor specification

There are three distinct boundaries. At the existing success amplitude, the target identity is implemented by the current comparator and is already present in the benchmark. At an upstream output block with singular value equal to the numerical probability, the desired probability requires a square-root transformation and a nontrivial phase sequence. At the financial arithmetic boundary, a meaningful encoding competitor must replace one or more emitted nonlinear tables or arithmetic components and then certify the resulting residual and its representation error afresh. These are different experiments and cannot share an unexplained query unit.

For a complete encoding competitor I retain the exact target scalar, interval and binary scaling; the state-preparation gates; all signal and inverse tapes; actual phase factors and their construction; polynomial parity and continuum magnitude certificate; a target-error certificate on every attainable finite input or on the whole reachable interval; independently certified Clifford+T phase tapes; all conjunction predicates; the parity-dependent good event; the corresponding Grover reflection; and the full estimator, observations and reset. The existing financial deterministic allowance and statistical confidence must be shared with the comparator, rather than supplemented after the calculation. Phase approximation is a deterministic bias of the encoded probability, not simply a reduction of the estimation confidence. In the simplest valid envelope, allocate $2\eta+2\xi$ to its normalised bias, where $\eta$ certifies the amplitude polynomial and $\xi$ certifies aggregate gate norm error. A sharper direct probability certificate can replace $2\eta$.

For this cap the two smoothed payoff terms depend on $m_A$ and $m_G$. Their difference is positive, but subtracting their square-root amplitudes does not produce the required difference of probabilities. A proposed two-signal construction must show the exact residual identity, its normalisation and its success probability before its cost can be compared. A Hadamard test of a signed difference is possible in principle, but introduces an offset and a distinct range; its cost and priors must be recomputed rather than assigned those of the existing small positive flag.

The Rall–Fuller estimator provides a separate possible next comparator. The statistical repair must spend failure probability over every adaptive confidence look, or use an anytime-valid confidence sequence; degree changes alone do not count all looks in its pseudocode. Its amplitude tolerance must be converted rigorously to the present probability tolerance using the current prior. The full finite maximum-history cap must be established or clearly distinguished from sampled controller histories. The empirical constants in that source are not admissible substitutes for these quantities.
