## 2 Attachment audit

I read the four supplied proposal and critique attachments, with independent mathematical and literature audits. Their principal binding corrections are the need for a constructive evaluator, geometric-control opponents, matched monetary accuracy, complete costs, conservative interpretation of synthetic data and a valid finite-representation certificate. Continuous-versus-discrete total variation is one, so I explicitly replace the earlier inappropriate small-TV suggestion with coupling or quadrature.

The local attachment hashes and provenance are retained in [attachment_manifest.json](attachment_manifest.json). The attachments are supplied analyses rather than independent academic evidence. I do not copy them into the repository or treat their unverified source claims as published results.
