# 1. Verdict and claim boundary

I identify a substantive project-specific contribution candidate: a computable financial-grid error certificate for a conditioned basket Taylor residual which retains dispersion, uses actual rounded equal-CDF Gaussian nodes, and avoids both a continuous price input and Cartesian enumeration. The displayed argument is a complete mathematical construction. The positive cell identity underlying it is exactly covered by Cerone (2002); its correctness does not establish originality. I do not yet identify it as a proved first contribution to the literature. Literature originality requires a comparison of the resulting theorem and its assumptions with close antecedents, including those whose full text remains unavailable.

The quantum development is a coherent evaluator of an established classical pricing residual, with a sparse payoff-boundary kernel and positive encoding when the payoff is convex. It develops a quantum pricing method within an existing amplitude-estimation framework. It does not invent a new quantum-mechanical asset model, a new generic amplitude-estimation primitive, or a new no-arbitrage theorem. The complete resource findings are original experimental results for the declared compiler and models; they do not themselves demonstrate algorithmic priority or practical quantum advantage.

# 5. Completed source and matched comparison

Cerone, P. (2002), “Weighted Three Point Identities and their Bounds”, *SUT Journal of Mathematics*, 38(1), 17–37, final [publisher PDF](https://www.rs.tus.ac.jp/sutjmath/_userdata/38-1/02-cerone.pdf), Theorem 3, equations (2.13)–(2.15), and Theorem 5, equation (3.21), pages 23–30. The choice n=1, alpha=a, beta=b, x=c and w=phi reproduces the centred first moment and both nonnegative second-order kernel branches exactly. The exact MGF then follows by testing the established identity on an exponential. These mechanisms cannot support a claim to original generic transport theory. Full source mapping and the version consulted are retained in certificate_source_comparison.md and certificate_source_access.json.

The same-grid R2 comparison uses identical nodes, norms, dispersion information and full tails in both orders. At each of five transported-coordinate partitions, the second-order certificate admits 33 allocations and the strengthened FTC certificate admits 32. Finest second-order allowances are 25.7–72.3 per cent smaller. Refinement from one block to 1,024 changes the second-order bounds by only 0.0702–0.3480 per cent and rescues no allocation failure. Both constructions factorise other coordinates; the cellwise endpoint is not a Cartesian enumeration. Cauchy refinement is established mathematics, and these outcomes do not support a new hierarchy theory. Joint geometry/both-order timings identify the additional work of this implementation, not an order-specific cost advantage.

The positive result is narrower: a fully computable residual derivative closure gives useful aggregate financial-grid allowances and supports the retained coherent implementation comparisons. This is a substantive project-specific result and original numerical evidence for the frozen compiler/model cohort. I have not established that this residual-specific conclusion is absent from the wider literature, or that no established approach obtains it at comparable cost. I therefore retain it as a contribution candidate, not an established original theorem. The three global-envelope failures and all stronger classical price certificates remain retained.

# 6. Remaining contribution question

I regard the construction as more than an untested proposal because its financial closure, emitted quantum composition and common-information outcomes are independently checked. A new proof of an existing conclusion would matter only if its different mechanism, stronger scope, sharper guarantee or reduced construction cost were material and demonstrated. The present comparison excludes novelty of the generic identity, supports residual-specific usefulness and rejects additional value from this block hierarchy in the tested cohort. It does not establish top-journal publishability.

The remaining priority question is whether the residual-specific degree-four envelope closure supplies an extra financial conclusion beyond established conditional-polynomial, basket-bound and Gaussian-integration methods at comparable complete cost. The unread Ju, Curran and Dingeç–Hörmann final details and a compatible QSP/modern-estimation comparison remain explicit gaps. Strong classical competitors resolve all 36 prices, so a quantum-breakthrough claim remains unsupported.
