# 1 Current research decision

I retain an established classical financial model and develop its quantum pricing method. The completed full-circuit comparison favours direct second-correction evaluation over the integral representation in every active matched case. I therefore use direct evaluation as the preferable second-correction implementation within this finite search. The integral construction remains a derived, implemented and audited alternative; its adverse outcome is retained.

# 2 Positive checked finding

Analytical Gaussian second-correction preconditioning and finance-specific signed priors reduce complete canonical-estimation T caps against the strong positive first-correction comparator in three active cases. The reductions are approximately 5.5, 20.8 and 59.9 per cent. The implementation selected in two of those cases uses rare positive/negative flags; the third uses the centred flag. The moment and monetary certificates supply the same information to both second-correction methods and a competent mean prior to the first correction.

A stronger Beta-cell and strike-midpoint derivation lowers one auxiliary error allowance from about 0.00936219 to 0.0000222520 without changing the circuit. The complete precision study retains 97 programs, 11,872 search decisions and all rejected allocation choices. Every selected width map and complete estimator cap has an independent audit.

# 3 What is and is not established

The new coherent value constructions and their conditional monetary guarantees are complete for the admitted two-asset representation. Higher-dimensional value circuits and finite moments remain mathematically and arithmetically supported, but their current continuous-price transfer does not meet the target. The strongest classical price policy already resolves all 27 frozen cases without quantum estimation; the validated two-asset financial transfer itself uses a classical price reference. I do not infer practical quantum advantage or theoretical priority from a correct circuit, a precision saving or a smaller logical cap.

The condensed main paper keeps the essential mathematical derivations and a focused appendix. The supporting index preserves the full original paper, additional proofs, source versions, gate plans and earlier studies. Further contribution work should address stable direct remainders, more efficient financial state preparation and stronger compatible quantum baselines, under a financial model where an equally informed classical price certificate does not already resolve the task.
