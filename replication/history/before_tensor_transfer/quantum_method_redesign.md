# 2 Implemented curvature corrections

The payoff-specific boundary oracle integrates the strike analytically and uses one Beta auxiliary. Convex finite piecewise-affine payoffs have a nonnegative second correction; the ordinary call uses its native positive probability. The stable Gauss-Jacobi opponent avoids raw-price subtraction. Raw R2 and positive R1 are fully charged competitors. Shared sharp M1/M2, finite fourth/sixth moments and price-independent financial transfers strengthen every method.

# 3 Retained finding

The complete pool has 173 candidates and 23,772 allocation decisions. Raw R2 wins all eight active quantum second-correction comparisons and beats positive R1 in five cases, with reductions of approximately 13.9 to 91.2 per cent. Boundary R2 also beats R1 in those five cases, but loses to raw R2. Both strong classical evaluators certify all twelve completed two-asset prices. Zero-shot prior decisions are analytical results. These are conditional logical-resource comparisons, without a practical quantum advantage or theoretical-priority conclusion.
