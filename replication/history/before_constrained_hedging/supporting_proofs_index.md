# Supporting proofs and evidence

## 1 Reading order

I use the [main paper](generalisation_proofs.tex) for the financial question, essential derivations, comparison and interpretation. Its appendices retain the main theorem proofs. The documents below supply extended constructions and implementation details. I distinguish mathematical correctness, logical resource evidence and literature priority.

The [originality assessment](originality_assessment.md) governs current claims. The paper is a working paper, not an original quantum theory or a completed demonstration of practical advantage.
