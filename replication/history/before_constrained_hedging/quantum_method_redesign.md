I use the project primarily to demonstrate mathematical and computational preparation for quantitative finance graduate study. The [preparation audit](PREPARATION.md) maps proofs/code to entry requirements and published modules, distinguishing applied depth from broader foundation gaps. A new quantum theory, publication or physical advantage is not required for this purpose. I must still explain the mathematics, implement the methods and interpret the financial comparisons accurately.

I retain the classical no-arbitrage financial law and quantum pricing investigation. The [working paper](generalisation_proofs.tex), [supporting index](supporting_proofs_index.md) and [originality assessment](originality_assessment.md) govern the checked results and literature boundaries. Frozen protocols, failures, comparative costs and historical source versions remain preserved.

## 2 Next preparation development

It links replication, the Black–Scholes PDE, martingale/call-spread valuation, backward-Euler stability/convergence and analytic/PDE/Monte Carlo prices and all five Greeks. Synthetic comparisons retain finite-domain, grid, interpolation, bump and sampling distinctions. A bounded mixed-gamma comparator strengthens the sampling baseline; adverse control rankings and non-monotone Greek refinement remain visible. The compiled companion and reproducing code/results are linked from the README. General ODE/PDE preparation, empirical model adequacy, broader inference and realised hedging remain separate gaps.

The projection/control step is complete in the same companion: full L²/L¹ conditional-expectation proofs, singular multivariate controls, independent-pilot excess risk and GBM conditioning comparisons. It retains unequal evaluator costs, the dominant analytic baseline, non-commuting optimal coefficients and adverse pilot rankings. These establish mathematical preparation and project-specific checked reasoning; they do not prove a new quantum theory or equal-cost superiority.

The Gaussian-estimation step is complete in the companion, with likelihood/rank proofs, exact marginal inference, corrected physical-drift moments and non-monotone price-uncertainty propagation. Its synthetic evidence does not establish empirical adequacy; the conditional statistical/numerical certificate remains unimplemented. The next selected priority is constrained hedging with an explicit financial objective, declared loss measure, convexity/existence arguments and KKT conditions, retaining unconstrained and no-hedge comparisons. Broader analysis/probability preparation and an original small C++ implementation remain separate priorities.

If I pursue their cost contribution, I must supply every applicable raw-payoff, numerical conditional-price and shared-threshold rival with the same price-independent information, register exact uniform-gap/root/error/selection rules before outcomes and rebuild complete financial allowances and cancellation-aware confidence charges. A smaller isolated derivative coefficient is not a complete cost improvement. If complete outcomes remain unchanged, I retain that result without presenting the refinement as a cost contribution. This optional novelty question no longer blocks the mathematical-preparation objective.
