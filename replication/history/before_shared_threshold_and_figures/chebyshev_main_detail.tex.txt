\section{A bounded Chebyshev estimator and its complete guarantee}
\label{app:bounded-chebyshev}

Let $A$ be the existing clean financial preparation, $p$ its good-flag
probability, $S_g=I-2P_g$, $S_0=I-2|0\rangle\langle0|$ and
$Q=-AS_0A^\dagger S_g$. With $p=\sin^2\theta$, the invariant
subspace gives $Q^kA|0\rangle=\sin((2k+1)\theta)|g\rangle+
\cos((2k+1)\theta)|b\rangle$. An odd degree $d=2k+1$ therefore
samples $T_d(\sqrt p)^2$ using $Q^kA$ and the good flag. For an even
degree $d=2k$, use $A^\dagger S_gQ^{k-1}A$ and the zero-input event:
\[
 \langle0|A^\dagger S_gQ^{k-1}A|0\rangle
 =\cos\theta\cos((2k-1)\theta)
  -\sin\theta\sin((2k-1)\theta)=\cos(2k\theta).
\]
Both parities use $d$ preparations or adjoints. The even sampler uses
$k$ good reflections and $k-1$ zero reflections; the odd sampler uses
$k$ of each. Every allocated wire is observed and reset. Uncontrolled
global phases are immaterial; the controlled fallback retains its phase.

I adapt the sampling framework of \citet{rallfuller2023}, Section~3.
A degree is admitted only when the current outward interval for
$t=2\arccos(\sqrt p)/\pi$ lies in one cell $j\leq dt\leq j+1$.
The cosine-square signal is monotone there, so validated inverse endpoints
and intersection preserve containment. Each look uses 100 fresh shots
and an exact fixed-binomial interval of failure allowance $\alpha/24$.
Counts are not pooled. I permit twelve looks and degree at most 2,047,
and accept a decoded probability midpoint only at radius
$\varepsilon=(0.01-D)/C$, where $D$ and $C$ are the retained
financial error and monetary signal factor.

\begin{proposition}[Coverage and complete worst-history charge]
Let the pilot's complete T budget be $B=\lfloor T_F/10\rfloor$, where
$T_F$ is the canonical fallback charge at failure allowance $\alpha/2$,
using the original deterministic financial prior. With fresh independent
ideal shots and the retained financial certificates, the bounded procedure
has complete monetary error at most $0.01$ with failure probability at
most $\alpha$, and every history costs at most $T_F+B$ T gates.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Conditionally on any preceding history, the next degree is fixed and its
fresh binomial interval fails with probability at most $\alpha/24$.
A union bound over at most twelve looks bounds all pilot failures by
$\alpha/2$. On their complement, outward inversion and intersection
contain $p$, so a successful pilot has monetary error at most
$D+C\varepsilon$. If it stops unsuccessfully, a fresh fallback spends
$\alpha/2$ against the original prior. It imports no sampled posterior.
The two failure events have union probability at most $\alpha$.
A batch is never run if its complete charge would exceed $B$; all earlier
failed work is retained. Thus pilot plus fallback costs at most $B+T_F$.
\end{proof}

For an explicitly specified shot law, let $S$ indicate successful pilot
termination and let $C_P$ denote its charged work. The complete charge is
$C_P+(1-S)T_F$, with no independence between $C_P$ and $S$ required.
Linearity of expectation gives the exact condition
\[
 \E[C_P+(1-S)T_F]<T_0
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \Pr(S=1)>1-\frac{T_0}{T_F}+\frac{\E C_P}{T_F}.
\]
With $T_F/T_0=27/25$ and $0\leq C_P\leq B$, success probability
above $2/27$ is necessary and above $2/27+B/T_F$ is sufficient.
The conservative sufficient threshold is $47/270$. This resource identity
is established probability algebra; its application identifies the pilot
success needed to repay the tighter fallback confidence and failed work.
A diagnostic mesh fraction is not the required success probability at the
unknown financial flag.

The expanded proof derives exact binomial coverage, root enclosures,
endpoint branches, parity circuits and observation/reset charges. This is
a finite implementation and financial composition of published mechanisms,
rather than a new generic estimator theorem. Diagnostic histories and
worst-history caps are distinct quantities.
