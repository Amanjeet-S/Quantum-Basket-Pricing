\caption{Different certificates under a controlled dispersion stress. Panel (a) uses the eight-asset ordinary call at $\lambda=0.2$, selected before outcomes. I divide the actual-grid financial-transfer allowance by $0.005$ and the complete Taylor-price intersection radius by $0.01$; the dashed line is admission at one. These are different error targets. Joined points guide the eye between tested scales. Panel (b) counts transfer-admitted contracts whose strengthened Taylor-price intervals fail, among all 36 contracts per scale. Degree five is evaluated only for provisional degree-three separators. All price intervals include lower-order intersections and intrinsic bounds. The figure reports certified allowances, not observed errors, complete quantum prices or a speed-up.}
\label{fig:dispersion-separation}
