# Application and comparison attacks after exact factor-basis checks

## 1. Priority-one conclusions

The exact rotation experiment is mathematically coherent, but it is not a new financial asset model. The continuous derivative target, no-arbitrage drifts, all asset covariances, the geometric coefficients and continuous ratio moments remain identical. Its positive evidence is confined to the registered ideal-logical pricing configurations and their retained synthetic financial inputs. A complete-resource reduction can be a useful project-specific numerical-finance result; it does not establish a new generic quantum algorithm, empirical asset-pricing result or practical advantage.

Every selected phase-register size, number of repetitions and Fourier tape is unchanged relative to the identity winner. The new selected T charges therefore improve through rebuilt financial preparations, not reduced algorithm calls. A description that says the derivative transfer reduction produced fewer quantum queries would be false. The paper should identify the direct financial-bound improvement and the full emitted-oracle improvement separately, and should state that their magnitudes differ.

The retained four continuous classical pricing certificates remain valid for the unchanged price target. Reusing them is mathematically justified by the proved continuous law identity. It is not a new price experiment under rotated numerical nodes. The evidence must not be relabelled as four freshly calculated rotated prices, four new empirical observations or a newly demonstrated quantum-versus-classical runtime comparison.

## 2. Classical perspective

For a deterministic fixed orthogonal matrix O and Y standard Gaussian, OY has exactly the law of Y. Therefore the scalar conditional payoff F_RO(Y)=F_R(OY) has the same entire probability distribution as F_R(Y). Its mean and variance are identical whenever they exist; for a capped contract they are bounded. An iid Monte Carlo estimator with the same independent standard Gaussian factors has the same ideal score variance and asymptotic confidence requirement. This proof is stronger than a numerical variance comparison, and removes any need to run redundant iid experiments solely to rediscover the invariant law.

The statement does not apply to a fixed finite tensor grid. Those factor laws are not rotationally invariant, so their score distribution and numerical price can change. Nor does it apply to QMC, coordinate-conditioned controls, sparse quadrature or optimised integration, where orientation changes effective dimension and numerical error. The new basis must be available to those classical opponents. A standard Gaussian factor transformation is a classical preprocessing tool even when the selected objective concerns a quantum circuit. The fact that it is compiled into a quantum preparation does not make the transformation itself quantum.

At present the identity and one certificate-selected rotation are fully costed per multi-factor case. All 58 direct certificates were screened, but the full T objective was not evaluated over all 58 rotations. The lowest direct error certificate need not produce the cheapest circuit or final resource cap. Calling the outcome a global optimal Gaussian basis would overstate the experiment. A registered finite-pool minimiser of one analytic certificate followed by a paired full-resource choice is the accurate description.

## 3. Financial perspective

The synthetic terminal lognormal model is defined under a risk-neutral measure, with an explicit risk-free rate, dividend yield, unit maturity and fixed spot and strike. A capped arithmetic-basket derivative is a coherent payoff in that model. Labelling the same inputs as REITs would add no evidence about real estate returns, market calibration, observed option liquidity or contractual availability. The present financial contribution is numerical pricing and error accounting under stipulated inputs.

The small cap relative to strike deliberately makes the common Gaussian smoothing and cash-Gamma behaviour important. It should not be presented as a representative market calibration. Increasing the number of assets in this factor family does not by itself establish classical computational hardness. In particular, the common-factor conditional expectation is already an effective classical variance reduction, and the retained classical integrations succeed on these cases. The project should describe the actual stress mechanism and the small finite population tested instead of treating dimensional labels as a computational-complexity result.

No new empirical financial data were used. All rotation matrices, Gaussian nodes, tables, probabilities, cost profiles and numerical results in this step arise from exact synthetic specifications. The university's LSEG licence is therefore not an obstacle to sharing this step's synthetic evidence. This does not authorise later redistribution of LSEG observations or reconstructible derived panels. No statement about a calibrated real estate universe or an institutional dataset should be added without actually acquiring permitted data and recording its provenance.

## 4. Improvement and exclusion criteria

The direct certificate, exact model law, rebuilt priors and complete T counts have now passed independent checks. A negative candidate is useful evidence and must remain in the record, particularly the rotated stable-Gauss all-12 failure that does not qualify for the boundary-only precision repair. Silently repairing every failing method would invalidate the frozen comparison. A basis cannot be accepted on the strength of a smaller loading-envelope sum while its arithmetic guard or monetary budget fails.

A stronger next oracle should be rejected if it reduces a pointwise normaliser by clipping an integrand whose actual range exceeds that normaliser. It should also be rejected if it swaps a continuous-factor identity for an unchanged finite-grid assertion, reuses the old implemented prior after introducing new sampling or arithmetic errors, or hides a new factor-law approximation in an uncharged flag. A screened analytic improvement is provisional until the complete coherent graph, arithmetic certificate, confidence rule and final emitted charge are reconstructed.

The project can improve its research relevance by clearly specifying where the method applies outside the exact four synthetic cases: bounded monotone payoffs admitting a layer-cake threshold representation; Gaussian conditional factors; exact rational factor rotations; and numerical integration methods with certified finite-law transfer. Such a theorem may establish applicability of a configuration, but priority must still be checked against published quantum Monte Carlo pricing, threshold-payoff encoding and Gaussian linear-transform literature. A broad novelty sentence is not a substitute for that comparison.

## 5. Audit disposition

The all-58 independent record checks 344 direct-transfer coordinates, 132,097 continuous multinomial exponent arguments, 83,592 derivative scalar inequalities and fresh common transport masses. A separate seven-basis record checks 333 financial-budget scalar identities, 256 implemented-prior scalar identities and 176 estimator target/prior identities. Every selected phase/repetition/Fourier profile remains unchanged. These records strengthen the reproducibility of this finite synthetic experiment. They do not convert it into a published theory, an empirical validation or physical-device evidence.
