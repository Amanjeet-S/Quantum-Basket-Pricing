## 2. Necessary minor fixes

The Blair source footnote contains `et al.\\'s`, which invokes an acute accent on the following s. Replace it with ordinary possessive punctuation or the clearer sentence: 'Only the publisher abstract of Blair et al. was accessible; its full proof-level comparison remains incomplete.'

In the ordinary-call remainder, define M_{k+1} as an upper bound on ||C_{k+1}||∞ at its first use; the general definition occurs later in Section 6. The revised notation otherwise correctly resolves the previous continuous-moment conflict.

The newly explicit optional uniform-box law needs the prepared-score link. The boundary equation defines the unweighted score X=2p−1, so its uniform-box prior cannot literally assert |X|≤omega U²/U_*² when omega<1. Define the weighted prepared score X_omega=omega X and its signed parts, with omega=1 for the retained quantile law, or keep the main formulas restricted to that actual law and refer the weighted variant to supporting material. Its weighted zeroth moment must remain E_N omega.

The short sketch after the signed-kink boundary theorem calls the second remainder positive. Positivity is restricted to nonnegative kink weights. The nonnegative Taylor weight gives the sign under convexity and the absolute norm bound for signed kink weights; this formulation avoids suggesting that a capped-call second correction is positive.
