# 1. Scope and basis

I apply the university guidance supplied on 9 October 2026 to any LSEG data used in this project. The entitlement is for my individual study and research. I do not treat academic access, a private repository or a source acknowledgement as permission to redistribute the underlying data. Free public material has its own licence, which I check separately. OptionMetrics, new subscriptions and entitlement upgrades remain excluded.

# 2. Material excluded from redistribution

I keep raw LSEG observations and exports out of GitHub, including private repositories, and do not supply them to other third parties. This restriction also covers reconstructible transformations and systematic derived datasets, such as security-level price or return panels, repeated extracts, large fitted-parameter panels and data caches. I do not put licensed inputs in commit history, notebooks with saved output, attachments, cloud shares, external AI services or public build logs. Underlying licensed files stay in private local storage outside version-control directories. Ignore rules are an additional safeguard, not a licence permission or a way to remove existing history.

# 3. Code and limited academic outputs

I may publish the extraction and analysis code without credentials, raw observations or embedded licensed outputs. Such code runs against an individually authorised local data source; public tests and examples use synthetic or separately licensed public inputs.

The supplied guidance permits insubstantial portions of derived data when they have no independent commercial value and their distribution is non-systematic. Academic and non-commercial statistical summaries and research results in a journal paper or university course application may therefore be suitable outputs within those conditions. I assess the actual output and the cumulative disclosure, rather than assuming that every computed quantity is publishable. A large derived table, repeated snapshots or a figure from which a substantial underlying series can be recovered does not become permissible merely because it is labelled a summary.

Permission for a journal paper or university application does not automatically authorise every GitHub release. I assess a GitHub copy of an LSEG-derived figure or summary against the same insubstantial, non-commercial and non-systematic conditions. If that separate distribution is not clearly permitted, I keep the licensed-derived file local and publish the code and synthetic example instead.

I do not use this academic entitlement for an external-company project, internship or commercial service, including work undertaken during an academic term. For an uncertain output, I omit the licensed content until the institution or provider clarifies the relevant permission; the code and synthetic experiment can remain available.

# 4. Figures, tables and attribution

I identify whether each figure and table uses synthetic inputs, independently public data or permitted LSEG-derived summaries. For a permitted LSEG-based result I state the relevant LSEG product, instruments or scope, observation period, access date and my calculation method. I do not invent a product or cite LSEG for inputs that did not come from it. Attribution does not authorise prohibited redistribution.

I retain the input lineage and licence basis locally. A research caption reports statistical results or aggregate diagnostics, not a disguised data export. Reproducibility for a licensed-data exercise means publicly available code and methods which another separately entitled researcher can run locally; it does not mean that I supply that researcher with the observations or a systematic derived dataset.

# 5. Acquisition and local processing

I use only the minimum task-defined extracts supported by my existing authorised entitlement. I do not perform systematic bulk downloads, repeated universe-wide backfills or automated collection intended to build a redistributable database. Licensed observations are processed locally and do not appear in shared outputs or external-service uploads. Extraction scripts and logs avoid credentials and raw records. Provider demonstration files may support format checks but do not count as an independent empirical validation sample.

# 6. Retention and completion

On completion of the project for which they were obtained, I delete the underlying licensed observations, unnecessary derived working datasets and their working copies or caches. I record the scope and date of that cleanup without republishing the data. I retain only code, permitted non-commercial research summaries and non-reconstructive provenance needed to document the analysis. No deletion is triggered merely by drafting this policy while the project remains active.

These LSEG-specific restrictions do not require deletion of independently generated synthetic model inputs, simulation traces or software-verification evidence. They remain clearly distinguished from licensed observations.

# 7. Current evidence

The current financial experiments use stipulated rational synthetic GBM parameters, controlled synthetic exposure stresses and generated numerical or circuit-verification results. The reviewed figure families derive from those inputs. They do not contain LSEG market observations. Public Sobol material retains its separate source licence. Publisher PDFs and the full supplied source attachments remain excluded from repository distribution; source-version and section records are retained instead.
