# Data provenance and publication review

## 1 Scope and conclusion

I apply the university guidance I received on 9 October 2026. I do not treat that guidance as a new permission to redistribute licensed records or as a legal opinion on the complete contract. I checked the current working tree, the principal financial input generators and protocols, the exact stress transformation, and the source hashes of the three current figure families. I found no LSEG observations or LSEG-derived empirical input in the checked chains. The exact generator reproduces all nine base-model dictionaries, and all 36 stressed models retain their stipulated inputs under exact rational scaling. The 144 payoff cases and the present figure coordinates therefore have synthetic origins established by code and retained provenance, rather than assumed from filenames.

## 2 Distinct materials

The Gaussian cells, moments, Taylor prices, error certificates and circuit resource counts are computed from stipulated synthetic inputs. The RQMC labels and scores arise from seeded digital shifts evaluated on those synthetic financial graphs. The ideal estimator simulation uses explicitly declared synthetic amplitudes. The public Sobol direction-number file and accompanying implementation are author-distributed mathematical material with their own retained licence and source record. They are neither LSEG data nor market observations. The source hashes linking dispersion, Taylor and QPE plot coordinates to the installed result files all match.

The current non-code CSV is the synthetic QPE resource case table. I found no local data/private directory, environment-file names or private-key filenames in the inspected working tree. This is an inventory observation, not proof about other directories, remote artifacts or deleted Git history.

## 3 Application of the university guidance

Raw LSEG data must remain outside GitHub and outside every third-party disclosure, including a private repository. Renaming, rounding, normalising or calibrating records does not itself establish redistribution permission. Systematic derived panels, fitted parameter histories, model-ready data exports and figures that effectively expose the underlying series should likewise remain local under the supplied guidance. Extraction and analysis code can be retained without embedded observations, credentials or exported responses.

Academic papers and university applications can present limited research results and statistical summaries as described by the university. Before including an LSEG-derived figure or table, I should identify the exact source, fields, sample period and transformations, check that the output is insubstantial and non-systematic and has no independent commercial value, and cite LSEG at the relevant result. I have no blanket numerical threshold that defines those terms. A repository supplement containing many individually small outputs could collectively form a systematic derived dataset. A GitHub licence on project code cannot grant rights to provider data.

Acquisition must stay within existing authorised access, with no new purchase or entitlement and no systematic bulk downloading. Underlying licensed data should be deleted at completion of the project for which it was used, including working copies and caches within the project lifecycle. That deletion should be recorded without publishing the data. I have not deleted anything during this review.

## 4 Paper and reproducibility wording

The live paper currently describes a future LSEG illustration as a publicly reproducible calibration exercise. I recommend replacing that wording with a code-reproducible workflow using separately entitled local inputs and permitted limited academic summaries. The current synthetic figures can retain their exact plotting data and source hashes because they do not encode LSEG observations. Future empirical figures need their own provenance and disclosure assessment; the synthetic classification does not carry over merely because the plotting code is unchanged.

I have edited no repository file, made no commit, published nothing, downloaded nothing and deleted no data.
