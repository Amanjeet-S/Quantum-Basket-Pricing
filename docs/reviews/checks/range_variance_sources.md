# 1. Variance source ledger

The direct derivation in range_variance_proof.tex uses the established mean-dependent range variance inequality and optimises it under upper first and second moments. It is not presented as a new generic variance theorem.

Bhatia, R. and Davis, C. (2000). 'A better bound on the variance'. The American Mathematical Monthly, 107(4), pp. 353–357. DOI: [10.1080/00029890.2000.12005203](https://doi.org/10.1080/00029890.2000.12005203). The publisher's final article record and the primary JSTOR issue record were retrieved and verify the authors, title, journal, volume, issue, pages and publication year. The proof therefore stands independently and does not claim to reproduce a section of the unread final paper.
