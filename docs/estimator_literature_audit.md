# Published-estimator and geometric-control source audit

## 1. Access and versions

I used Safari for the published journal IQAE article and attempted the MIQAE publisher PDF. The latter redirected to purchase options, so I did not buy access. Further reading used accessible primary publisher content and author and institutional repositories.

Grinko, D., Gacon, J., Zoufal, C. and Woerner, S. (2021), ‘Iterative quantum amplitude estimation’, *npj Quantum Information*, 7, article 52. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41534-021-00379-1. I read the final publisher HTML in Safari, especially Theorem 1, Algorithms 1–2 and equations 6–10. I also read the final publisher Supplementary Information, dated 1 February 2021, Section 2 and Lemma 1, through the publisher PDF retrieval at https://media.springernature.com/original/springer-static/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41534-021-00379-1/MediaObjects/41534_2021_379_MOESM1_ESM.pdf. Its fifty coefficient is retained only for the source algorithm.

Fukuzawa, S., Ho, C., Irani, S. and Zion, J. (2023), ‘Modified Iterative Quantum Amplitude Estimation is Asymptotically Optimal’, *2023 Proceedings of the Symposium on Algorithm Engineering and Experiments (ALENEX)*, pp. 135–147. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1137/1.9781611977561.ch12. The publisher records publication on 12 January 2023. This is a published proceedings chapter, not a journal article. The final chapter full text was inaccessible in Safari. I actually consulted https://arxiv.org/pdf/2208.14612, version 4 dated 23 February 2023, Sections 2–3, Algorithms 1–2 and Lemmas 3.1–3.4. The full-batch choice is explicitly described in Section 2.1. I do not imply that the publisher’s final chapter text was read.

Curran, M. (1994), ‘Valuing Asian and Portfolio Options by Conditioning on the Geometric Mean Price’, *Management Science*, 40(12), pp. 1705–1711. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.40.12.1705. I verified the final publisher metadata and abstract. The abstract supports geometric conditioning as an antecedent; I did not read the final full article in this audit and make no detailed proof-level attribution to it.

Korn, R. and Zeytun, S. (2013), ‘Efficient basket Monte Carlo option pricing via a simple analytical approximation’, *Journal of Computational and Applied Mathematics*, 243(1), pp. 48–59. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cam.2012.10.035. I read accessible indexed primary publisher HTML for the abstract and Sections 1–4, including the text around Theorem 1. Direct full-page retrieval returned403. Some mathematical formulae were missing in that rendering. The displayed text identifies an additive-shift C→∞ arithmetic/geometric limit and a shifted-lognormal approximation used as a control variate. This prevents an unrestricted novelty claim about arithmetic/geometric limiting arguments; it does not establish that the exact centred-loading L2 theorem is already present.

Dingeç, K.D. and Hörmann, W. (2013), ‘Control variates and conditional Monte Carlo for basket and Asian options’, *Insurance: Mathematics and Economics*, 52(3), pp. 421–434. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.insmatheco.2013.03.002. Publisher page: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0167668713000309. I consulted the primary publisher abstract and introduction, which describe a control based on an expected payoff conditional on the geometric average exceeding the strike. Full article equations were not read in this audit. Its general method is an established antecedent; detailed novelty comparison remains open.

Dingeç, K.D., Sak, H. and Hörmann, W. (2015), ‘Variance Reduction for Asian Options under a General Model Framework’, *Review of Finance*, 19(2), pp. 907–949. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1093/rof/rfu005. The final publisher abstract at https://academic.oup.com/rof/article-abstract/19/2/907/1580917 identifies a control applicable to models with a computable multivariate log-return characteristic function, and conditional Monte Carlo for Lévy, Heston and regime-switching models. The article appeared online on 6 March 2014 and in the March 2015 issue. I read the final publisher abstract and metadata, not its inaccessible full text. This is a direct antecedent for a general transform-based financial control framework.

Montanaro, A. (2015), ‘Quantum speedup of Monte Carlo methods’, *Proceedings of the Royal Society A*, 471(2181), article 20150301. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1098/rspa.2015.0301. The University of Bristol identifies https://research-information.bris.ac.uk/files/108674307/20150301.full.pdf as the final published version; that PDF retrieval returned 403. I subsequently accessed the complete final published article on PMC at https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4614442/ and read Sections 2(c)–2(d), Algorithms 3–4 and Theorems 2.5–2.6. The supplied variance or relative-variance upper bound is a premise of those algorithms. They require coherent numerical output and its inverse, rather than merely a Bernoulli flag with the same mean.

## 2. Comparison conclusions

I retain the journal IQAE theorem as the primary published confidence comparator and the published MIQAE chapter as an additional source, with its author-version access limitation. The rational-radius code is a separately proved MIQAE-informed controller. Its finite inverse padding and integer caps are explicit; neither published numerical coefficient is silently transferred to it.

The literature establishes geometric conditioning, geometric controls, arithmetic/geometric limiting approximations and bounded-variance quantum mean estimation. A defensible wider contribution must concern an explicit transferable financial certificate and the resulting error/encoding comparison, rather than rename those ingredients. The available access does not support a categorical claim that no earlier paper contains the exact proposed moment or weak-dispersion result.
